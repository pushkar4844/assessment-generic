Opening Introduction

AWS Cloud Internet Breakout Assessment Call

The Intro

First 2 minutes — set the tone
"Hi everyone, thank you for taking the time today. My name is Pushkar Patil — I'm a Cloud Engineer with AWS Professional Services. I specialize in network architecture and security for cloud environments, and I've been working with customers on AWS infrastructure design, security posture assessments, and internet breakout architectures."
"Before we jump into the technical details, I want to take a couple of minutes to set the context for what we're doing here and how we'll work together."

Build Trust — Show You Understand Their World

Next 3 minutes
"I understand you're looking at your AWS Cloud Internet Breakout environment — how your workloads connect to the internet, how that traffic is secured, and whether the architecture is aligned with best practices. This is one of the most critical areas in any cloud environment because it's where your internal workloads meet the public internet. Getting this right has a direct impact on your security posture, compliance readiness, and operational resilience."
"I want to be upfront about how we approach this — this is NOT an audit. We're not here to grade you or find fault. We're here as your partner to give you a clear, honest picture of where things stand today, and more importantly, a practical roadmap to get where you want to be."
"Every organization has gaps — that's normal, especially as cloud environments grow and evolve. What matters is knowing where they are and having a plan."

Establish Credibility — Frameworks

Next 2 minutes
"Our assessment is built on the same frameworks AWS uses with enterprise customers globally:"
"First, the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework — specifically the Security Perspective. This gives us the organizational view — governance, identity, threat detection, infrastructure protection, incident response. It's how we measure security maturity."
"Second, the AWS Well-Architected Framework — Security Pillar. This is the workload-level lens. It looks at how your architecture decisions impact security. Together with CAF, we're covering both the big picture and the details."
"For your internet breakout specifically, we'll benchmark against the AWS Security Reference Architecture — this is AWS's prescriptive design for how network traffic should flow through centralized inspection, egress filtering, and segmented VPCs. It's the gold standard."
"And for configuration hardening, we'll use the CIS AWS Foundations Benchmark — the latest version. This gives us 39 automated controls that Security Hub can continuously monitor after we're done."
"So you're not just getting our opinion — you're getting an assessment grounded in industry-recognized standards."

Set Expectations — What They Get

Next 2 minutes
"Here's what you'll walk away with at the end of this engagement:"
  • "A scored assessment workbook — every area rated on a 1-5 maturity scale with Red, Amber, Green status. You'll see exactly where you're strong and where the gaps are."
  • "A findings tracker with severity ratings, business impact, and specific remediation steps — not generic advice, but actionable recommendations tailored to your environment."
  • "An executive summary dashboard your leadership can use to understand the security posture at a glance."
  • "And a prioritized remediation roadmap — because fixing everything at once isn't realistic. We'll help you sequence what matters most."

Make It Collaborative — Ask Them

Next 2 minutes
"Before we dive in, I'd love to hear from you:"
"What's driving this assessment? Is it a compliance requirement, a security concern, a leadership initiative, or are you planning changes to your architecture?"
(Let them talk. Listen. This tells you everything about their priorities.)
"Are there specific areas you're most concerned about or want us to focus on?"
(This makes them feel heard and gives you the priority order.)
"Who are the key stakeholders we should involve — network team, security team, identity team, operations?"
(This shows you respect their org structure and want to include the right people.)

Close the Intro — Transition to Assessment

Wrap-up
"Perfect. That's really helpful context. Here's how I'd like to structure our conversation today — we'll start from the foundation and work our way through layer by layer:"
"We'll begin with your account structure and governance — how your AWS environment is organized. Then we'll move into network architecture and the internet breakout path. From there, we'll look at the security controls on that path, identity and access, data protection, monitoring, and incident response."
"Each layer builds on the previous one, so by the end we'll have a complete picture."
"I'll be taking notes as we go and mapping everything to our assessment workbook. If at any point something isn't clear or you want to go deeper on a topic, just say so — this is your assessment."
"Ready to get started?"

Cheat Sheet — Trust Phrases to Drop Naturally

  • "We see this across many customers at your scale..."— normalizes their gaps
  • "That's actually a common pattern we encounter..."— removes shame
  • "The good news is this is straightforward to fix..."— gives hope
  • "AWS has a native service for exactly this..."— shows you know the platform
  • "Based on what we've seen work well for similar environments..."— positions you as experienced
  • "Let me show you what the reference architecture recommends here..."— grounds advice in AWS standards
  • "This is one of the highest-impact improvements you can make..."— helps them prioritize
  • "Your team has done solid work here..."— acknowledge what's working, don't just find problems
