Field SA Guide — Diving Deep: Technology Landscape Discovery Questions

Strategy

  • How does the system support the customer's business? (e.g. CRM logs customer interactions and integrates with ERP for fulfillment)
  • How does the system scale as the business grows? What are the growth constraints? (e.g. servers, storage, people)
  • How does the system handle business continuity?
  • What are the technologies used in the system? (e.g. Oracle, OpenJDK, Javascript, Linux, VMWare)
  • How does the system integrate with other systems? (web hooks, integration broker, etc)
  • What are the peak performance metrics for the system? When does the system require peak performance? (e.g. maximum transactions per second, maximum tenants per cluster, etc)
  • What teams support the systems? (e.g. development, operations, DBA)
  • How does the team build and deploy the system? (e.g. Gradle, Jenkins, Ansible)
  • How do the teams test the systems? (e.g. manual, automated functional regression, unit tests, integration tests)
  • How does the team monitor the system for security, application, and performance incidents?
  • What are the system's incident response processes?
  • How do the team predict failure or service level violations? (e.g. AIOps)
  • How do you support the end users of the system? (e.g. ticketing systems, call/contact center)
  • How could the system generate more value if augmented with AI/ML?
  • Could the system's data provide insights to increase efficiency, drive growth via cross/upsell opportunities, etc.?

Technology Landscape

  • How does the technology organization serve the business? Are there dedicated teams for each business unit? Does a single team serve all the business units?
  • What regulatory constraints do you have? (e.g. HIPAA compliance, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) audits, FINRA compliance, etc)
  • What drives the buy vs build decision? (e.g. customer buys accounting and HR systems but builds development tools)
  • Do you have an AI and ML strategy for augmenting or automating your business processes? (automating document workflows, information retrieval from unstructured data, etc)
  • Do you have a strategy to derive business insights from your data? (e.g. a "Customer 360" strategy by pulling data from CRM, customer success, and ERP system into a single view and data lake)
  • Do you have a strategy to monetize your data? (e.g. improving credit quality decisions using payment data, improving rating models through driver telematics, predictive maintenance program through IoT and service history)
  • What is your overall application integration strategy? (Enterprise service bus/integration broker (Mulesoft, Informatica, Boomi), point to point, etc.)
  • What is the software delivery process? (Scrum, Agile, XP, Waterfall)
  • What do you use for development tools, databases, application monitoring tools, security monitoring tools, continuous integration (CI), continuous delivery (CD)?
  • What percentage of technology infrastructure is in the cloud? What percentage is in AWS?
  • Does your IT strategy explicitly state cloud's role and how it is tied to your business objectives?
  • Where do you want to invest in infrastructure – innovation vs. application outcomes?
  • Are you aligning strategy on both cost out and innovation?

Security

  • What are the security compliance requirements? (SOC2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, etc)
  • Does your organization have a security and compliance program?
  • What tools or services are being used for security control?
  • What regulations does your organization need to comply with?
  • How would you characterize your organization's approach to security and compliance? Do you find it effective and efficient at managing risk?
  • To what extent are you incorporating data analytics into your operational risk management program?
  • Are there risk and compliance management practices that you feel would benefit from a more data driven approach?
  • What are the most resource intensive areas of your security and compliance program?
  • What are the main cost drivers, and where do you see opportunities for increased automation of manual processes?
  • At a high level, how would you characterize your organization's approach to security and compliance? How integrated and consistent would you say it is across lines of business?

Opportunity Information

  • Do you currently have central pane of glass view into your AWS security posture?
  • Do you currently or plan on storing or processing protected data?
  • What 3rd party products are you currently using on premise / on AWS?

Analytics

  • What type of data are you working with and where does it come from?
  • Are these data structured, unstructured, or semi-structured?
  • What you will want to do with the data? E.g. store now analyze later, or operational reporting
  • How are you currently capturing and storing your data? What tools and databases are in use?
  • What is your strategy for data management and governance?
  • How complex is your data acquisition process?
  • What is your current ETL process?
  • What type of skills exist in your organization? E.g. DBA, IT engineers, big data engineers
  • How do you currently manage data?
  • How does all this go with your organization's culture?
  • What are your plans to address regulation?
  • What are the top pain points of running your existing data analytics environment?
  • Are there particular areas where you would like to apply analytics or AI/ML technologies (but haven't yet)?

Opportunity Information

  • Are you looking to migrate some or all of your data analytics workload to the cloud?
    • If so, what are your specific requirements and timeline?
  • Do you have partners to help you with the migration?
  • Would you be interested in knowing AWS data lake and analytics offerings?
  • Would you like to do a migration evaluation for your existing data analytics to AWS Redshift or data lake?

Backup/DR

  • Do you currently have a Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)?
  • What is the overall RTO and RPO defined?
  • What tools and/or vendors are you using for DR?
  • Can you describe your DR processes in general?
  • How often do you conduct DR test or exercises?
  • Can you tell me your most recent DR experience?
    • Areas of focus: Policies
    • Procedures
    • Organization
    • RTOs and RPOs (business vs technical)
    • Date of DRP last updated
    • DR exercise frequency and last exercise date
    • Are staff trained on DRP
    • Does everyone understand their roles within the DRP
    • Are there any SLA considerations, if so, what are they?
    • Are there any security considerations, if so, what are they?
  • What is the backup solution being used in your environment?
  • How much data are you protecting in your environment?
  • Where are you storing the backup data today?
  • Can you describe your backup processes in general?
  • How often do you run backup jobs?
  • What are the main applications and data type you are backing up?
  • Are you sending your backup data offsite or to the cloud?
  • Is your DR based on restoring from backup data?
    • Areas of focus: Backup architecture
    • Backup policy (source data, frequency, retention etc.)
    • Backup full capacity (breakdown by files, databases, applications, etc.)
    • Backup medium: disk, tape, cloud, offsite?
    • Backup window
    • How often do you test recovery from backup?
    • Are there procedures for recovery from backup?
    • Organization structure
    • Do you offer SLAs to business groups?

Opportunity Information

  • Are you interested in discussing ways to modernize your backup process without changing your workflow using the AWS cloud?
  • Are you using a backup vendor with a connector that allows backups to be written to S3?
  • Would you be interested in knowing AWS backup and DR automation solutions?
  • Would you like to do a POC using AWS backup and DR services?

Databases

  • What database technology is currently supporting your applications?
  • What applications are running on top of your databases? Please name them specifically.
  • How large is this workload(s) in terms of infrastructure requirements? Servers, storage, networking, support staff, etc.
  • How many corresponding design, development, test, QA, staging, pre-production, disaster recovery environments are you running?
  • What middleware software are you running alongside the database systems? E.g. WebLogic, HTTP server or listener, identity management, etc.
  • Are you running into any performance issues or management overhead?
  • How many databases do you run today? Which databases are they and their versions?
  • How many DBAs manage the databases? How are they keeping up with growing number of databases?
  • What is the RTO and RPO for the databases?
  • How long do the database data need to be queried?

Opportunity Information

  • What are the top pain points of running your existing database environment?
  • Do you have a robust disaster recovery plan for your critical databases?
  • Have you considered using cloud for DR?
  • Have you thought of migrating off proprietary databases and option open source alternatives?
  • Would you like to do a migration evaluation for your existing databases to AWS RDS?

Data Transfer

  • How much data do you plan to transfer to AWS?
  • What is your internet bandwidth?
  • What is the ETA for the transfer?
  • What are the sources of the data transfer?
  • What are the targets of the data transfer? E.g. S3, EFS, FSx, EC2, RDS, etc.
  • Any preference on data transfer protocols? E.g. FTP, SFTP, FTPS, etc.
  • Can you provide a list of your existing file servers and NAS devices and their OS versions and models?
  • Can you provide a list of your existing block storage devices and their models?
  • Can you provide a list of your existing object storage devices and their models?
  • What is your existing backup solution and backup storage in use? Is backup data deduped?

Opportunity Information
